# Flavorium Universalis (Balance) — Imperial Examination Privileges
#
# The keju examination order, expressed as two estate privileges. The exam
# was a narrow, gruelling meritocracy that in practice locked advancement to
# the scholar-gentry. Kiss-curse: strong administrative / scholarly bonuses,
# paid for with a pop-promotion-speed malus (only the exam-passing gentry
# rise; everyone else stays put).
#
# Seeded onto Chinese-region AI at game start (and onto break-away /
# released Chinese states mid-game) by cc_caste_exam_seed.txt.
# The player dismantles them via the cc_keju.* reform chain, which sets
# cc_keju_dismantled and revokes them; the guard in potential keeps them
# from being re-granted afterwards.
############################################
