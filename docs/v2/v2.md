# Cabinets and Choices — v2.0 Design Overview

## The Question v2.0 Answers

v0.1 asked: *what kind of cabinet member do you have?*
v2.0 asks: *what happens between your cabinet members, and between your cabinet and the world?*

The cabinet is already a living thing — it grows, decays, synergizes, leaves legacies. v2.0 deepens that by adding interpersonal dynamics, explicit ministerial roles, progressive character growth, and connections to war, religion, succession, and diplomacy.

---

## What v0.1 Already Has (Do Not Duplicate)

- Cabinet traits: 140+ across Tiers 1–3, age-specific, conditional, negative, legendary
- Subject types: 23, with advance-gated unlock chains and five-dimensional bond tracking
- Synergy events: single/dual/multi (up to 5) cabinet combinations; international synergies
- Age traits: feudal (Age 1), 34 arc traits (Ages 2–6)
- Flavor: HYW, Hussite, AI personality evolution, wealth hoarding
- Legacy system: philosopher_king, grand_vizier, tribune_of_the_people + mentorship/retirement

## Recommended Implementation Order

### Tier 1 — Foundation (start here)

These three can be done independently and unlock or deepen everything else.

1. **DONE****[Pillar 11 — Cabinet Duties](pillar-11-cabinet-duties.md)**
   - Pure new cabinet action files; no existing files modified
   - Creates the throttle mechanism other pillars need
   - Low risk of breaking existing systems

2. **DONE****[Pillar 9 — Auto-Modifiers](pillar-09-auto-modifiers.md)**
   - Pure data in `common/auto_modifiers/` — no events to write
   - Immediate payoff: cabinet composition finally has aggregate effects
   - Reuses every existing trait; zero modifications to existing files

3. **DONE****[Pillar 12 — Stepping Stone Traits](pillar-12-stepping-stone-traits.md)**
   - Define new Tier 0 traits + progression events early
   - Later pillars can assume chains exist and reference them
   - Modifies existing `allow` blocks in `cabinet.txt` — do this before writing new pillar events that reference those traits

### Tier 2 — Core Gameplay Loops

4. **DONE****[Pillar 4 — War Council](pillar-04-war-council.md)**
   - Natural companion to Duties (cc_duty_war_council)
   - Hooks into existing military traits (no new traits needed beyond Chain D in Pillar 12)
   - Gives players immediate impact from cabinet during wars

5. **DONE****[Pillar 2 — Estate Factions](pillar-02-estate-factions.md)**
   - Introduces Family G (category = health traits); only tiny modifiers
   - Faction balance events are the real payoff
   - Complements Pillar 9 auto-modifiers

6. **DONE****[Pillar 1 — Court Rivalry](pillar-01-court-rivalry.md)**
   - First interpersonal system; reuses all existing conditional traits as rivalry seeds
   - Chains trigger from societal-axis opposition (already dense in v0.1)
   - Delivers high drama for relatively low new content volume

### Tier 3 — World Expansion

7. **[Pillar 3 — Scholar's Court](pillar-03-scholars-court.md)**
   - Small, focused; extends Age 5–6 which is already populated
   - Introduces the Royal Academy building (new system, low risk)

8. **[Pillar 5 — Succession Crisis](pillar-05-succession-crisis.md)**
   - Hooks into regency/on_new_ruler on-actions
   - Moderate scope; high historical payoff

9. **[Pillar 8 — Congress System](pillar-08-congress-system.md)**
   - Extends existing intl synergy infrastructure
   - Small scope; pairs naturally with diplomatic trait chains from Pillar 12

### Tier 4 — Deep Content

10. **[Pillar 6 — Religious Tensions](pillar-06-religious-tensions.md)**
    - Large scope; Age 4 is the most event-dense age already
    - High historical depth; implement confessional traits first, then events

11. **DONE****[Pillar 7 — Colonial Cabinet](pillar-07-colonial-cabinet.md)**
    - Requires v0.1 subject types to be working correctly
    - Can be partially implemented (company events only, posting mechanic later)

12. **[Pillar 10 — Historical Flavor](pillar-10-historical-flavor.md)**
    - Fully independent; implement one region at a time (Ottoman → Italian → Spanish → Mughal → French)
    - Each sub-chain is ~100–200 lines; low risk
