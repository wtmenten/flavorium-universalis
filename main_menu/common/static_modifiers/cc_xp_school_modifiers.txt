###############################################################################
# Flavorium Universalis — Cabinet Experience: school modifiers
#
# QUALITY SCALES THEM. Every seat, kind and curriculum modifier is added with
# size = cc_xp_school_N_qscale (0.5 at quality 0, 1.0 at 50, 1.5 at 100), so the values
# below are what an average school gives. Renown has its own country modifier, sized by
# the realm's most renowned school.
#
# SEAT AND CURRICULUM modifiers are not added once and tracked. cc_xp_school_maintain
# re-adds them every month for 2 months with mode = replace, so they follow the school:
# a seat that is lost or a school sent into exile simply stops being refreshed, and the
# bonus lapses on its own. Curriculum changes strip the old ones at once
# (cc_xp_school_strip_modifiers) so the switch shows immediately.
#
# Two schools on the same curriculum share one country modifier: a realm gains each
# curriculum's national bonus once, however many schools teach it.
#
# ACTION modifiers (library, scholars) are timed and stay where they were applied.
###############################################################################
